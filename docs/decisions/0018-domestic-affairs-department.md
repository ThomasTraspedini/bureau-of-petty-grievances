# 0018 — Domestic Affairs department

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Department expansion must make shared-household irritation precise without turning the Bureau into a domestic monitoring, hygiene, food, property, or safety authority. A third complete path also verifies that department-aware filing, assessment, localized generation, presentation, persistence, sharing, and analytics remain genuine product boundaries.

Household habits could invite photographs, sensors, room maps, named locations, inferred occupancy, or continuous observation. Those sources are unnecessary and disproportionate for harmless grievances. Bounded facts deliberately entered by the filer can create specificity without constructing a record of the home or claiming external verification.

## Decision

The Department of Domestic Affairs is the third enabled end-to-end department. Its first policy version supports exactly three classifications:

- token remainder in a shared container: remaining servings and total container capacity;
- object stopped near its proper location: object count, correction distance in steps, and plausible correction effort in seconds;
- empty packaging returned to storage: empty-package count and recurrence within the preceding 30 days.

Values are bounded structured facts entered by the filer. The Bureau accepts no photos, sensor data, home maps, room names, household monitoring, or external investigation. A bounded witness statement remains submitted context rather than verified evidence.

Domestic Affairs owns deterministic severity thresholds, one capped consequence adjustment, required mitigation, stable presentation parameters, and three non-binding remedy families: finish the shared-container handoff, complete final object placement, or remove or plainly mark empty packaging. Remedies cover no more than three future shared-household occasions. They do not govern emergencies, safety, access needs, caregiving, health, work duties, hygiene, food access or restriction, property disposal, financial penalties, deprivation, exclusion, or public humiliation.

The determination uses a domestic property register distinct from the Chronology timeline and Digital Conduct communications docket. Depending on the classification, it renders a container gauge, a correction path, or inventory markers from approved submitted measures and labels the filer as the evidence source. Department-specific English editorial guidance and deterministic fallback share the existing language-neutral determination schema.

Draft and transient-session envelopes advance to version `3` with validated migration of version-1 Chronology and version-2 department state. Share descriptors advance to version `3`; Domestic previews disclose only remaining and capacity servings, object count and correction distance, or empty-package count and recurrence. They exclude correction effort, respondent alias, witness statement, room names, relationship, impact, and generated prose. Analytics advances to schema version `3` with the Domestic evidence step and three categorical paths, never submitted fact values. PostgreSQL schema version `5` admits the `domestic_affairs` department and `DOM` reference prefix while retaining existing records.

## Consequences

- Three departments now complete the same filing-to-public-record lifecycle with separate fact and visualization grammars.
- The property register can feel exact without implying observation or authority over the home.
- Existing device-local work and transient determinations continue through explicit migration rather than silent loss.
- Provider, preview, analytics, and persistence boundaries retain separate validated allowlists.
- Future Domestic classifications must remain harmless, bounded, filer-submitted, and free of surveillance or serious household governance.

## Open considerations

Social Planning remains required for the Department expansion capability. Any Domestic feature involving media upload, sensors, connected devices, location maps, household members who are minors, safety or accessibility disputes, caregiving, health, work performance, hygiene enforcement, food control, property disposal, or financial sanctions requires a separate material decision before implementation.
